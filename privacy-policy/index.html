Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit PANKRI and how the browser-based learning tools handle values you enter.

Last updated: November 3, 2026
Important scope note

PANKRI is delivered as a static website in this release. The included pages do not use a WordPress application, analytics tracker, advertising script or third-party form processor. Your hosting provider and email provider may still process technical or correspondence data as described below. This policy must be updated if you later add analytics, advertising, consent tools, forms or other services.

1. Information processed by the visualizer

The sorting, searching, graph, data-structure and Bloom Filter demonstrations are designed to run in your browser. Values you enter into the tool are processed on your device by the page's JavaScript. This project does not intentionally send those input values to a PANKRI application server or store them in a PANKRI database.

The page stores your selected light or dark theme preference in your browser's local storage so the preference can be remembered on that browser. You can clear this data using your browser's site-data controls.

2. Server and technical information

When any website is requested, the website host and network providers may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the pages, such as IP address, request time, browser and device details, requested URL, and error or security logs. The exact information and retention period depend on PANKRI's hosting provider and configuration. These server-level practices should be confirmed against your hosting account's terms and settings.

3. Information you send by email

If you contact PANKRI at contact@pankri.com, your email provider and PANKRI's email service will process the message and the contact details included in it. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information by ordinary email. PANKRI may retain correspondence as needed to respond, handle corrections or maintain business records, subject to applicable requirements.

4. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

The current PANKRI website code uses browser local storage only to remember the selected light or dark theme. This package does not include analytics or advertising scripts and does not set tracking cookies. Your hosting provider may use necessary server-side logs or security measures. Review this policy again if you add any third-party technologies.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

Google AdSense advertising code is not included in this Algorithm Lab project package. If PANKRI enables AdSense or another advertising service in the future, advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalize ads, subject to their own policies and applicable settings. Update this policy before enabling advertising so it accurately identifies the providers and choices available to visitors. More information about Google's advertising technologies is available in Google's Advertising Policies and Technologies information.

6. Third-party websites and services

Pages may link to external websites such as LinkedIn or Google's policy pages. PANKRI does not control how those external services collect or use information. Review the privacy policies of third-party services you choose to visit.

7. Children's privacy

The visualizer is an educational resource and does not require an account. PANKRI does not use this tool to knowingly request personal information from children. Parents or guardians who have concerns about information submitted to PANKRI can contact us using the address below.

8. Your choices and questions

You can clear the visualizer's saved theme preference through your browser settings, choose not to send an email, and manage cookie settings through your browser. Additional rights or choices may apply depending on your location and the services enabled on the wider website.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the tool or the wider site's data practices change. The date at the top indicates when this page was last reviewed. Please revisit it periodically.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact contact@pankri.com.
