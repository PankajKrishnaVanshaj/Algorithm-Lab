What can you contact us about?

  • Tool issue: Tell us which visualizer you used, what steps you took and what happened.
  • Content correction: Share the article or section URL and explain what should be checked.
  • Suggestion: Recommend an algorithm, data structure or learning feature you would find useful.
  • Business or collaboration: Include a short description of your proposal and relevant details.
  • Privacy concern: Use the email address above and include “Privacy request” in the subject.

What to include in a bug report

  1. The exact page URL.
  2. Your browser and device type.
  3. The input or sample data used, provided it contains no private information.
  4. The expected result and what actually happened.
